The Project

Hyperformula Music is an independent music project built around creative turntablism, human musicianship, experimentation, and hybrid music production. The goal is simple: create music without forcing it into one box. Electronic production, live performance, sampling, sound design, and human expression can all exist in the same space.

Creative Philosophy

Human & Hybrid

Technology is part of the process, but human creativity remains at the center. Production can move between physical performance, digital manipulation, sampling, synthesis, and experimentation.

Creative Turntablism

Turntables are treated as instruments rather than simply playback devices — another way to manipulate rhythm, texture, movement, and sound.

No Fixed Box

Genres can overlap, collide, and disappear entirely when the music calls for it. The production follows the idea rather than forcing the idea to follow a genre.

The Music Comes First

Every release is ultimately about the listening experience: the sound, the atmosphere, the emotion, and the connection between the artist and the listener.
